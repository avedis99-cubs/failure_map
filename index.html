Does the dram fall when Armenian prices rise?

Our income is in dollars, most of our spending in drams. If Armenian prices rise 1% faster and the dram holds steady, our cost of living in dollars rises 1% every year — forever. If the dram falls by that same 1%, it costs us nothing.

What the reserve decision costs

Ring-fencing the care reserve is not free. Carving $386,124 out of the portfolio and refusing to spend it on anything else costs about 0.9pp of required return against no reserve at all, and the severe level costs over 4pp — beyond what any portfolio delivers. Switch How it is carried to in the cash flow to see the same care paid as it falls due: cheaper on paper, because the money stays invested, but it offers no protection against the reserve being spent on something else.

Whether the retirement year matters

It does now. Across the employment options the line runs from 3.76% with no employment to 3.09% working to 2036 — a span of 0.67pp, and working to 2036 beats stopping in 2032 by 0.24pp. Earlier versions of this map showed the date as near-neutral because surplus income in 2033–36 was discarded rather than banked; it is banked now. The earnings test costs less than it appears, because the months withheld are largely recovered at full retirement age.

The one lever we control

Switch sequencing from optimal to naive and the line jumps about 1pp — as much as the widest currency scenario. It is the largest single variable entirely within our hands. Everything that moves the line further is either a medical-cost assumption, the size of the care reserve, or a fact about Child 2 we do not get to choose.
